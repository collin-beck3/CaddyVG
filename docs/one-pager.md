# CaddyVG

CaddyVG is a virtual golf caddie that delivers "plays-like" yardages by combining GPS distance, elevation changes, real-time weather, and wind conditions into a single shot recommendation.

CaddyVG is a mobile golf companion designed to help everyday golfers navigate the course with the confidence and strategy of a professional caddie. Unlike traditional GPS and rangefinder applications that provide only raw yardages, CaddyVG incorporates real-time weather conditions, wind speed and direction, and elevation changes to deliver adjusted yardages that reflect the true difficulty of each shot.

With detailed hole layouts, hazard awareness, and green elevation maps, golfers receive the information they need to make smarter decisions from tee to green. By combining course data with environmental factors, CaddyVG acts as a virtual caddie in the palm of your hand, helping players select the right club, avoid costly mistakes, and approach every shot with confidence.

Whether you're a weekend golfer or a competitive player, CaddyVG simplifies on-course decision-making, allowing you to spend less time calculating and more time focusing on executing your next shot.

This application includes: 
- Raw distance -> “plays-like” yardages 
- Elevation adjustment
- Temperature adjustment
- Wind adjustment
- GPS distance
- Hazard awareness 
- Virtual Scorecard to track score
